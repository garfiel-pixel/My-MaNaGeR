Pricing

One free plan that never expires. One paid plan that lifts a single limit.

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Refund Policy · Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

The short version

My MaNaGeR works fully offline and always will. The free plan is not a trial and does not run out. Paying is optional, and nothing important is locked behind payment.

Premium removes one thing and one thing only: the limit on how many projects you can link to cloud backup.

Free plan

Price: nothing, for as long as you want it.

The free plan includes everything the app does:

  • Every feature, with no time limit. Nothing is held back for a trial period, and no feature disappears when a trial ends.
  • Unlimited projects on this device. Work offline, save to the device, export to a file. No account needed.
  • Up to 8 projects linked to cloud backup. Cloud backup is optional. If you do not sign in, there is no limit to reach.
  • The Build Cost Calculator, at full price. Including the Jamaica rate book, printed estimates and CSV exports.
  • The AI assistant and the MCP connector. Bring your own AI key, or use the free ones already built in.

To start on the free plan, open the app. Nothing to sign up for.

Premium plan

Price: $18.99 per month, billed monthly. Cancel any time.

Premium does not add features. It removes a ceiling. If you link more than 8 projects to cloud backup, the app will ask you to upgrade before you create the next one.

  • No limit on cloud-linked projects. Link as many as your work needs.
  • Everything on the free plan stays exactly as it is. Same features, same calculator, same assistant, same offline use.

To upgrade, sign in and use the Upgrade button on your projects page. Checkout opens in a new tab.

How billing works

  • Payments are handled by Paddle. Paddle is the merchant of record: they process your card, calculate and remit any sales tax, and handle billing support. Your card details never touch our servers.
  • You are billed monthly. There is no annual plan and no long contract.
  • Cancel any time. Cancelling stops the next payment. Your Premium access runs to the end of the period you already paid for, and the free plan takes over after that.
  • Cancelling does not delete anything. Your projects stay in your account and stay readable. Premium only controls how many of them you may link.
  • Taxes are handled for you. Any tax due is calculated by Paddle at checkout from your location.

Refunds

If Premium is not right for you, ask for a refund within 30 days of a payment and we will put the money back. The full policy, including what is and is not covered, is on the Refund Policy page.

What Premium will not do

We would rather be blunt than sell you something you will regret:

  • Premium does not make the app work better. The free plan has every feature. There is no "premium-only" tool, model or export.
  • Premium does not add more cloud storage per project. It removes a count, not a quota.
  • Your data stays yours either way. Cancelling or never paying does not affect projects already on your device. As always, keep your own backups.

If eight linked projects is enough for your work, the free plan is the right choice and we would rather you stay on it.

Questions

  • Do I need an account to use the app? No. Everything works on the device without signing in. Signing in only enables cloud backup and moving projects between devices.
  • Can I switch back to free later? Yes, at any time. See Refunds for what happens to the projects already linked.
  • What if I want more than one plan? There is only this one. If your work needs something we do not have, tell us on the Contact page.
