[AGENT -> SUPERVISOR] 2026-10-26 - MAT-3 SHIPPED (uncommitted, with MAT-2): PRICES THAT CHANGED AFTER A
LINE WAS ADDED. The owner's report: a line priced earlier keeps its old numbers when rates change, and
nothing says so. It must never change silently - that would rewrite a quote someone may have sent.
- Every bill line is stamped ONCE, when it is first saved (that is the moment it was quoted): pricedAt
  (the date) plus rateSig, a short signature of the PRICING SOURCE it was quoted from. The source is
  read the way D4 reads it - a field that still equals its prefill marker came from the book/model and
  FOLLOWS it, an empty field is priced by the source, and anything typed by hand is the owner's own
  money that can never move. So the signature carries the book/model row per following field (through
  modelRatesFor, the same call the prefill makes) and the exchange rate where the MAT-1 allowances
  convert at price time. A line whose every figure is hand-typed gets a signature nothing can move, and
  never flags.
- When the source moves: the line total stays EXACTLY as it was, and a note appears under that row -
  "Prices changed since 12 Oct" with the only two honest answers: Re-price this line (takes the current
  figure for the prefills, writes both marker keys so it reads as a prefill and never as "your rate",
  touches nothing hand-typed, re-stamps that line and no other) and Keep as quoted (hides the note for
  that signature; a later change raises it again). Legacy lines carry no stamp and the bill claims no
  date for them - we do not know when they were priced, so we say nothing.
- The bill and the CSV export carry the priced date (the export only when what is exported IS a line on
  the bill - an estimate that was never quoted claims no price date at all).
- Gate: tools/qa-calc-prices-changed.cjs 9/9, registered CI and wired into ci.yml T4 next to MAT-1/2.
  It proves the whole matrix in a real browser: stamp once, rate change moves the source not the line
  (second line on the bill provably untouched), Re-price moves only that line, Keep as quoted with no
  money moving and a third change raising it again, hand-typed line silent, legacy line unstamped, and
  the export row - which was the one bug I hit in my own harness (csvQuote quotes every field, so the
  first regex looked for an unquoted row and reported false).
- CSS: .bcp-boq-flag sits UNDER its own row (the row is one flex line with Edit/Remove on the end, so
