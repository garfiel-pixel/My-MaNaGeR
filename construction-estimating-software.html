The My MaNaGeR Rate Book

The built-in rate book is quoted in Jamaican dollars and covers the trades a contractor prices every week. Search a rate, pick a trade, set the quantity and the line is costed in the same workspace that runs the project.

  • Searchable rates. Find a line by trade or description instead of scrolling a static sheet.
  • Unit-aware quantities. Area, volume, length and count are handled with the right unit key.
  • Estimate to budget. Approved lines flow into the project budget, so the plan and the price stay aligned.
  • Custom lines. Add your own rates when the book does not cover the work.

Who it is for

Estimators, quantity surveyors and contractor-owners in Jamaica who want a fast first price, then a clean hand-off to the project team without a second spreadsheet.
