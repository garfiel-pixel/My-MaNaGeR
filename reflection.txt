LESSONS
  - When CI stops early, the remaining steps are UNVERIFIED, not passing. A green
    local verify plus a green qa-full said nothing about the fifteen suites CI
    skipped - and those suites were holding two real user-facing defects.
  - Read a red suite before re-baselining it. Twice in this wave the "stale
    harness" assumption was wrong in the opposite direction: once the harness was
    right and the app was wrong (expiry, contrast), once the harness was wrong
    and the app was right (W11, qa-shortcuts).
  - A mutation proof must rebuild. Pages load dist/mmgr.min.css and dist/*.js, so
    a source mutation without node build.js proves nothing. The proof was the
    broken thing, not the gate.
  - Two consumers of the same number must call one helper. "N days out" existed
    twice, computed two ways; they now share daysUntil.
  - PowerShell cannot host inline node -e with regexes or quotes. Write scripts
    to tmp/*.cjs. This session lost two calls to that trap.
