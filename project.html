    Do I need an account or an internet connection?
    No. Everything saves to your browser (LocalStorage) and works fully offline. You only need a connection for optional extras: the Cloud AI tier with your own key, live weather forecasts, voice caption downloads, and Google sign-in sync.
    Is my project data sent anywhere?
    No. The Local AI tier runs entirely in your browser, and nothing uploads unless you opt in. The Cloud AI tier sends only the compiled project-context prompt to the provider you choose, using your own key , the key and the data are never stored on our servers and never included in project exports or backups.
    How does the built-in AI work , and do I need my own key?
    Two tiers. Local runs zero-key, offline, and every line it writes traces to a real field in your project. Cloud takes your own key for OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude , pick the provider, paste the key, and Connect & Test verifies it live before Send unlocks. The AI window keeps a Chat / Presets tab bar at the top: the conversation lives under Chat, and the one-click prompt chips live under Presets , always one click away, no matter how long the conversation gets.
    What happens if my AI provider rate-limits me?
    The Cloud tier automatically retries on the next smaller model from the same provider , for example Gemini 2.0 Flash → Flash Lite → Flash Latest , and shows an amber "Fell back to …" badge in the chat naming the model that actually answered. Only capacity rejections (HTTP 429/503) trigger the fallback; a rejected key (401/403) is cleared immediately rather than quietly retried on smaller models.
    Where does my AI key live?
    In this browser tab only (sessionStorage), for this session. It is cleared when you close the tab or press Clear, and it never appears in project state, exports, backups, or the error log.
    How do I back up or move my project?
    Use Generate Output to export a timestamped .json file and re-import it on any device. Optional Google Drive backup/restore and sign-in sync are available too , but the export file is the guaranteed path on every device, signed in or not.
    Can I use this on my phone or tablet?
    Yes , the workspace is responsive, installs like an app (PWA), and keeps working offline after the first load. Voice capture runs on the device too.
    How does voice capture work?
    Dictate a site meeting and whisper transcribes it offline in your browser , no cloud, no per-minute fee. Recorded disputes feed straight into the Claim Pack.
    What is a Claim Pack?
    Weather delays and schedule slips compiled into a written delay argument with liquidated-damages exposure , incurred vs defensible , ready for counsel review.
    How do I share updates with the team or client?
    From the Controls tab: Copy As (Slack / email / client formats) and ready-to-send email templates (status update, change request, risk escalation, closure sign-off). Export the .json for a full handoff.
