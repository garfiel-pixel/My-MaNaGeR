How offline-first works

The workspace runs entirely in your browser and stores your project on your device. There is no server in the loop, so a dropped connection never blocks a task update, a cost entry or a daily record.

  • No account required. Open the app and work; sign-in only unlocks backup and moving projects between devices.
  • Per-project sync. Turn cloud sync on for a project when you want it, and leave it off for everything else.
  • Portable data. Export a project as a file and carry it to another device or a backup drive.
  • Works on modest hardware. A modern browser is the only requirement.

What stays live without a network

Scheduling, the Kanban board, budget and earned value, risk and issue registers, claims, meetings and the daily field log all keep working offline. Weather-aware features use cached data and degrade gracefully when there is no signal.

Why it matters on a Jamaican site

When the signal drops in the middle of a pour or a walkthrough, the record should not stop. Offline-first means the person closest to the work can capture it immediately, and the office sees it when sync resumes.
