What the workspace covers

My MaNaGeR takes a construction project from the first charter to the final closeout binder in one place. Each panel feeds the next, so the schedule, the budget and the claim trail never drift apart.

  • Charter and WBS. Define scope, deliverables and work packages, then break them into tasks with owners and dates.
  • Gantt and Kanban. See the plan as a schedule or as a live board, with dependencies and critical path.
  • Budget and EVM. Track cost to date, forecast to completion and earned-value variance against the same task list.
  • Risks, issues and decisions. Log them once and let them flow into reports instead of separate spreadsheets.
  • Claims and digests. Turn recorded delays and weather into a traceable claim pack when a position needs defending.
  • Closeout. RFIs, submittals, punch lists, COIs, permits and warranties each carry their own lifecycle.

Who it is for

Jamaican general contractors, project managers, superintendents and trade leads who need one source of truth on site. The app runs in the browser, needs no server, and works when the network does not.

Why offline-first matters here

Site connectivity is unreliable, so the workspace is local-first by design. Your data lives in your browser and keeps working with no signal; cloud sync is an optional switch per project, never a requirement.
