Compare estimates

Rates are planning-grade averages for early estimates, not quotes - expect roughly 15 percent under to 30 percent over actual until drawings are priced. Every line item is editable in the app once the project starts. Tax rates are the standard national rates (PwC VAT/GST table, 2026) - a custom rate field overrides them. Dimensions can be typed in metric or imperial - the math is the same underneath. Everything runs on this device; clearing your browser data clears the history, the bill and saved estimates.
