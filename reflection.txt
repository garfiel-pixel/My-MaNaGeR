2026-10-18 (audit Sprint 1): the hard lesson of this session is that a
correct fix and a green harness are two different things, and only one of them
is negotiable. The C2 refusal broke 20 page gates and 7 assistant gates; every
one of them had recorded the OLD lie as the expected value. Rewriting an
assertion to match new behaviour is right, but only when the new behaviour is
the thing the owner asked for - so each rewrite carries the reason in a
comment, and each red gate got a probe before it got an edit. The page harness
is 247/247; the assistant harness is still 98/7 and I did not paper over it.
Second lesson: a raw `select.value = 'USD'` is not a currency change. The
engine keeps its own state, so the harnesses spent months silently pricing on
whatever currency the previous gate left behind.

2026-10-18 (S1 close): two lessons worth more than the fixes. One: when a gate
goes red after a correct change, the first question is not "how do I make the
gate pass" but "was this gate ever testing the right thing". Here seven
assistant gates looked like one story (state leaking) and were actually three
stories, and one of them was a real bug I had introduced - the fail-closed
quantity guard refused good lines because it re-derived a number the engine
legitimately adjusts. Two: a document is a different artefact from a screen.
Whole units are right on a card you glance at and wrong in a file someone adds
up. That one distinction, two decimals on the way out, is most of C3.
