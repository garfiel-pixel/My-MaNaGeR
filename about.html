About My MaNaGeR

One workspace for running a construction or capital project end-to-end, built for the people who actually build things, and local-first by design.

Every drawing, budget line, and meeting minute, in one local-first workspace.

What it is

My MaNaGeR is a single-page workspace for running a construction or capital project end-to-end, charter through closeout, without stitching together five different tools. Eighteen panels, one project, zero tab-switching. A built-in AI engine runs zero-key and offline, with an optional bring-your-own-key Cloud tier (OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude) that auto-falls back to a smaller model whenever a provider is rate-limited.

Why it exists

Project software shouldn't need servers, subscriptions, or a training course, and the people who run the job shouldn't re-enter the same numbers into five different tools. My MaNaGeR exists so the schedule, the budget, the risk register, and the weather all talk to each other: one source of truth, always in reach, even offline.

What matters to us

  • Your data stays on your device. Local-first by design.
  • Field reality matters: weather, lead times, and punch lists are first-class.
  • The tool should get out of the way of running the project.
  • Copy-ready outputs. Digests, agendas, and reports you can paste anywhere.
