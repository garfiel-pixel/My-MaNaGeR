THE DEBUGGING LESSONS THAT MATTER
  - REPRODUCE THE RUNNER, DON'T ARGUE WITH IT. privacy.html passed locally
    at 320 in every configuration I could build. The fix came from
    CALIBRATING a proxy: force Verdana, confirm it reproduces the runner's
