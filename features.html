WBS + Gantt

Schedule

The work-breakdown structure is the spine everything else attaches to. Build it once, and the Gantt, Kanban, RACI, and Dashboard all stay in sync.

  • WBS Text Import: paste a plain-text outline; blank lines start tasks, indents become subtasks, and re-pasting merges by name instead of duplicating.
  • Dates cascade automatically from predecessors, with a cycle guard so circular dependencies can't lock up the math.
  • Critical Path Highlighter lights the deadline-driving chain in gold; Deadline Pulse flags any task within three days of its end date.
  • Lead-Time tasks track submitted and expected dates for permits and procurement; Recurring tasks auto-span the whole project.

Kanban Board

Execution

Your WBS tasks, seen as cards across To Do, In Progress, Blocked, and Completed, with tools built for the field, not the boardroom.

  • Dedicated Lead-Time lane keeps permits and procurement items from cluttering the normal columns.
  • Populate Sprint clamps every in-progress task's dates inside your sprint window, a fast way to timebox the current push.
  • Dashed cards mark lead-time items; amber pulses flag tasks within three days of their deadline.
  • Sprint Planning, Review, and Retrospective templates unlock when you're running Agile.

RACI Matrix

Accountability

Rows and columns pull live from your WBS tasks and your people lists. Add a person or task anywhere else and it appears here with nothing to re-enter.

  • Click any cell to cycle Responsible → Accountable → Consulted → Informed.
  • Built from the same data as the Gantt and the Kanban, so there's no stale, hand-typed matrix.
  • Every handoff stays unambiguous, from kickoff meeting to closeout.

Risk + Monte Carlo

Forecast

A risk register and issue log that feed a real simulation engine, not a gut-feel forecast.

  • 1,000-trial Monte Carlo simulation computes hit probability and P50 finish dates on your target completion.
  • conf:high / conf:low tags from AI-generated dates shape each task's risk spread in the simulation.
  • Open, unmitigated risks weigh directly into the Dashboard's Health Score.
  • Float Watch and Crash Candidates rank recovery options, excluding work gated by permits or curing time.

Budget / EVM

Finance

Budget lines and a spend log roll straight into earned-value math. The forecast updates the moment the numbers do.

  • Auto-calculated SPI, CPI, EAC, ETC, VAC, and TCPI once you have a budget line and a dated task.
  • S-curve chart of planned vs. actual spend, always in view.
  • Labor-cost math uses your Work Week (3-7 days) from Settings.

Built-In AI, Not a Middleman

Intelligence

A real AI engine that runs inside the app with zero API keys and zero cloud dependency. It never invents numbers: every line it writes traces to a real field in your project.

  • Works fully offline. Reports, digests, and schedule audits compile from live project state.
  • One-click presets write results straight back into the project (a claim argument, a weekly digest, a status report), or copy the prompt for any external AI you prefer.
  • A dated WBS outline comes back through the Import Dates tool, so nothing touches your schedule before you review it.
  • Optional BYO-key Cloud tier for heavier reasoning. Connect OpenAI, Google Gemini, or Anthropic Claude with your own key; your live project data is compiled into the prompt and nothing else leaves the browser.
  • Automatic model fallback. If a provider rate-limits the current model (HTTP 429/503), the app retries the same question on the next smaller model in that provider's ladder and shows an amber "Fell back to …" badge naming the model that actually answered. A rejected key (401/403) is cleared immediately instead.

Voice Capture & Claim Pack

Field

Talk the minutes instead of typing them. Capture a site meeting by voice and get a transcript on the meeting record. It transcribes offline in your browser, with no cloud and no per-minute fee.

  • Live captions while you talk; an offline whisper transcription finalized when you stop.
  • Rule-based extraction pulls decisions and action items you confirm before the meeting closes out.
  • A recorded weather-stop or dispute becomes a counsel-ready Claim Pack. Slips, LD exposure, and decisions compile from your own records for a date window you choose.

Liquid Glass UI

Design

A dual-engine visual layer: the CSS glass default looks sharp on anything, and a premium Three.js tier renders true chromatic-aberration glass on capable hardware. Opt-in, never bundled, never a requirement.

  • Glass lives on the functional layer only (header, controls, sheets); content stays solid for legibility.
  • Respects reduced-motion and reduced-transparency settings. Decoration never wins over usability.
  • Circuit-broken by design: if the premium engine can't load, the UI falls back to CSS glass without a hiccup.

Weather-Aware

Field

Site forecasts and weather windows keep schedule reality in front of you, before the rain decides for you.

  • Weather Variance flags weather-exposed tasks that lack enough cushion against your rainy-season window.
  • Dispute-ready delay log tied to your exposed tasks, with dates and reasons on record.
  • Weather-season contingency feeds the Charter's target-date reasoning.

Offline-First

Privacy

Runs in your browser, autosaves to LocalStorage, and exports a portable file. Nothing uploads by default.

  • No servers, no accounts, no subscription, and no tracking.
  • Save Project to File / Load Project from File moves work between devices.
  • Access codes protect each project, with view-only codes for read-only review.

Backup & Sync, Account Optional

Portability

An account is a convenience, never a requirement. The app is 100% usable with zero sign-in, forever. Export a portable file and it opens on any device, signed in or not.

  • Save Project to File / Load Project from File. The guaranteed sync route on every device.
  • Optional Google sign-in unlocks Drive backup and cross-device sync for convenience only.
  • Field-level merge: edit the same project on two devices and the newest write wins per field, never a whole-file last-save.
