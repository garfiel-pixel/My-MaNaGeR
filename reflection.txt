WHAT WAS STILL BROKEN AND IS FIXED HERE

1. `js/mmgr-tour.js` step 1 had no spotlight. The selector list named
   `.app-sidebar`, but `project.html:1400` renders `<aside id="app-sidebar">`,
   an ID. A live probe at 1440x900 returned 0 elements for the class selector
   and 6 boxes for `#app-sidebar .nav-group`; the ring was `display:none` for
   the whole step, which is why `qa-first-project-tour` T2c reported
   `overlap:false` on exactly that one card. Fixed the selector to
   `#app-sidebar .nav-group` (compact `#sec-nav` fallback kept).

2. The same function placed the ring ONCE (rAF + a 420 ms settle). The tour
   auto-starts the moment `#sec-nav .sec-btn` exists, which can be before the
   desktop rail is laid out, so a step could stay unringed forever. `render()`
   now retries placement every 150 ms for up to 1.5 s while the target has no
   box, with `placeTimer` cleared on the next step and on `end()`.

3. The card's colours were broken in light theme. Line ~199 used
   `var(--panel,#15171b)` and `var(--tx,#e8eaee)`, and lines ~216/220 used
   `var(--tx-sub,#a0acbe)`. None of `--panel`, `--tx`, `--tx-sub` exists in
   `css/src` (probe: all three compute empty). The card therefore painted the
   dark fallback `#15171b` while the two muted strings resolved through a real
   token, `--text-secondary` = `#555555` in light: 2.4:1. The committed
   "contrast fix" (`ed0091a`) changed `--tx-sub` to `--text-secondary` and made
   it worse than the 3.12:1 `#666666` `ba0b024` measured. The card now uses
   `--card` and `--text`, both defined in `10-tokens.css` (light) and in
   `body.dark-mode` (20-shell.css), so it is a light surface with `#333333` text
   on a light page and `#151518` with `#f5f5f5` in dark. `--text-secondary`
   stays for the muted lines (7.4:1 light, 8.9:1 dark).

4. `tools/qa-drive-state.cjs` failed the whole browser-app job with
   `TypeError: fetch failed` at the `/json` line. The 60 x 300 ms wait for
   Chrome's DevTools port expired and the next fetch threw. It now waits 30 s,
   logs the binary it tried, kills and relaunches once, and only then raises a
   named error (`Chrome never answered on the DevTools port ...`). The nine
   contract arms are untouched - no assertion was weakened.
