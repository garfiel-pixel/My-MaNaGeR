WHAT WAS ACTUALLY BROKEN, AND WHY

1. `qa-procurement` T2 pinned `quotedBy === 'local (this device)'`. That value
   only ever appeared while the session resolved late; B1's boot-time session
   read (a183fee) makes `actorLabel()` answer with the account, so a signed-in
   price edit stamps `owner@example.com`. The gate now accepts either label and
   still rejects a blank stamp - 51/51. The harness routes `/api/auth/me` to
   that exact address, so the expectation follows the fixture it already owns.

2. `qa-consent` S1 then failed, because a red step hides every step after it.
   Cause is mine, from B7: `mmgr_rail_nudge` is written in `app.html:1987` and
   was never added to `docs/STORAGE-INVENTORY.md`. Row added. The inventory
   VERSION stays at 2 on purpose - `js/consent.js:49-54` says to bump on a
   CATEGORY change, the banner asks nothing about a necessary one-time flag,
   and re-asking every visitor a question whose answer cannot differ is cost
   without a privacy gain. 37/37.

3. `qa-calc-assistant` W11 dark hover failed on the next run. Not a colour
   defect: the first-run rail nudge slides the sidebar out 0.9s after load and
   moved the card out from under the pointer, so the gate measured the RESTING
   tone. Proven by probe: the same selector in isolation reports
   `rgb(46, 125, 50)` on hover. The harness now waits for `rail-nudge` to
   appear and clear, re-points until `el.matches(':hover')` is true, and fails
   with a named arm ("the pointer actually landed") if it never is - 157/157.
   This is the same bug class `f0c5ec7` fixed in `qa-appshell`; the lesson is
   now written into `EXECUTION-PLAN.md`.
